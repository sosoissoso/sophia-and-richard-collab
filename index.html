Did You Get Fooled?

Did you assume the color of each book based only on the text? Were you surprised to see that the book cover was not what you expected? Were you disappointed? Why were you disappointed? Was it because you judged something from a biased standpoint before you actually saw it?

This is a visual representation of how easily we can be tricked by our own assumptions and biases. The labels in the dropdowns are not what they seem. This serves as a reminder to question our first impressions, look deeper for the truth, and ask why we believed something in the first place.
